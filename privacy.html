Project Aster

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Project Aster is a free study app for students preparing for JEE. This page explains, in plain language, what information the app uses and why. The app is in beta, so this page may change as we add features.

Who we are

Project Aster is made and run by an independent developer. Contact: gamingastral727@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your study data. XP, streak, practice and mock test results, syllabus tracker ticks, your study plan and tasks, your mistakes notebook, settings and your daily goal. This is saved on your device. If you sign in, it is also saved in your account so it follows you to other devices.
  • Sign-in details. If you sign in with Google or with an email and password, we receive your name, email address, profile picture and a unique account ID from Google Firebase Authentication. We never see your Google password.
  • Community chat posts. Anything you post in the chat is stored with your display name, profile picture, the time and your account ID. Chat posts are public to everyone who uses the app. Do not share personal details there.
  • Feedback you send. The message, the type, an optional contact you choose to give, the app version and your account ID if you are signed in.
  • Anonymous usage counts. A random ID that is not linked to your account, the date, and how many times features were used (for example, opened the Practice tab). We use this to see what works. You can switch it off in Help & settings.
  • Technical data. Our hosting and database providers may record your IP address and browser details in their normal server logs.

Why we use it

To run the app, sync your progress, show the community chat, keep it safe (moderation), fix bugs, answer your messages and improve features. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who else handles your data

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) stores accounts, synced progress, chat posts and feedback.
  • Google Sign-In and Google Fonts load from Google when you use them.
  • Netlify hosts the website files.
  • cdnjs provides a code library used to create PDF files.

These companies process data under their own policies and may store it on servers outside India.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. You can delete your account and all of your data yourself in the app: Profile → Help, feedback & settings → Delete my account. This removes your sign-in account, saved progress and chat posts. Anonymous usage counts cannot be linked to you, so they are not removed.

Your choices

  • Download a backup of your data from the Stats page at any time.
  • Delete your account and data from the app, or email us and we will do it.
  • Switch off anonymous usage counts in Help & settings.
  • Ask us what we hold about you, or to correct it.

Children and teenagers

Many JEE students are under 18. If you are under 18, please use Project Aster with the knowledge of a parent or guardian. We collect only what the app needs. A parent or guardian can email us to have a child's data deleted.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS and protected by account-based access rules. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep chat posts free of personal information.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: gamingastral727@gmail.com.
